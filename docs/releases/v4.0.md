**SR Loom is now Stereo Loom**, and it is no longer only for Leia SR displays. It now lives under [Stereopticon](https://github.com/Stereopticon-Org) alongside wiz3D.

Settings, profiles and the log carry straight over from SR Loom. It is still a single `StereoLoom.exe`.

## New Features
- **Display Output.** A new section below Stereo 3D Input chooses where the 3D goes:
    * **Leia SR**, as before. Its settings now sit under it: Weaver, Anti-Crosstalk (method, strength and Contrast) and, where their software is installed, the Acer SpatialLabs and Samsung Odyssey 3D options.
    * **Zondision** displays such as the Zimo M1 (woven by the display itself).
    * **3D Vision**, through NV3D-Lib (3D Vision Restoration), for NVIDIA 3D Vision glasses and emitter.
    * **Anaglyph**, with a choice of glasses and colour method.
    * **Side-by-Side** and **Top-and-Bottom**, each in Half and Full. Full switches the display to a double wide or double tall mode where it offers one.
    * **Row interleaved, Column interleaved and Checkerboard**, for passive and DLP displays.
    * **HDMI Frame Packing** (1080p or 720p). It sets the resolution only, never the refresh rate.
    * **Katanga**, for sending the 3D on to Katanga (Katanga needs to be running first).
- **Without an SR display**, Stereo Loom offers another output instead of stopping.
- **Samsung Odyssey 3D options** (shown only where Samsung's Hub is installed):
    * **3D Popup** turns off Hub's fullscreen "convert to 3D?" popup.
    * **Games In SBS** sets Hub's own 3D games to show side by side, so Stereo Loom weaves them. Each game's own values are put back when it is turned off.
- **Gaussian Splats (experimental).** A look-around mode for stereo pictures that draws new views as your head moves.
- **New logo**, from the Stereopticon set. The tray icon follows Windows' light or dark taskbar.

## Other improvements
- Faster conversion for Pulfrich, frame sequential, 360°, RGB + Depth, the simple anaglyph modes, Checkerboard and Recovered Colour.
- HDR through the weave: the weave is made in the ordinary range and brought back after it.
- Profiles: `swap_eyes=auto`, field sequential taken from the title, display settings keys, and a three way format toggle.
- Panel: Straight to Display and Realtime GPU toggles, hotkeys for Convergence and Anti-Crosstalk with a small readout, wrapped tooltips and a slightly more compact expanded panel.
- Katanga: a picture that fills only part of its texture is cropped to it.

## Bug fixes

- Fixed the previously picked display carrying on showing after picking another.
